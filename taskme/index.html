Send tasks

One task, one person, one deadline

Creating a task takes a few seconds. Choose who it’s for, write what needs doing, and pick when it’s due.

  • Send it to anyone close to you

    Pick a friend or family member — or yourself, for your own to-dos.

  • Set a clear deadline

    Quick picks for today, tomorrow and the next days, or any date up to a year ahead.

  • Know when it’s done

    They accept the task and complete it in the app. You get notified the moment it’s finished.

Features

Simple by design

Everything you need to keep track of what matters, and nothing you don’t.

Reminders before the deadline

Turn on a reminder and TaskMe notifies you shortly before a task is due.

Status at a glance

In progress, completed, declined or expired — every task shows exactly where it stands.

Tasks for yourself

Use the same simple flow for your own to-dos, kept alongside the tasks you share.

Friends by TaskMe ID

Add people with their TaskMe ID. Only friends can send you tasks, and you can block anyone at any time.

Full history

Look back at every task you’ve sent and received, and how each one turned out.

Instant notifications

Know right away when you receive a task, or when one you sent is accepted or completed.

How it works

Up and running in a minute

No setup, no teams to configure. Just you and the people you trust.

Sign in with Google

Continue with your Google account and your TaskMe account is ready.

Add your people

Search for friends and family by their TaskMe ID and send a request.

Send your first task

Write the task, set a deadline and a reminder, and follow it until it’s done.
